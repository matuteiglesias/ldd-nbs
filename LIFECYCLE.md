# Repository lifecycle

**State:** `maintenance`  
**Decision date:** 2026-08-03  
**Review cadence:** annual  
**Next portfolio review:** August 2027

## Why this state

This repository is retained as a published collection of notebooks used in Laboratorio de Datos. Its value is educational continuity and access to the course material, not an open-ended commitment to modernize every notebook or dependency.

## Maintenance policy

- Preserve the notebooks and their pedagogical sequence.
- Verify the published notebook site and a representative local notebook annually or before reuse in a course.
- Correct broken navigation, environment instructions, or material errors that affect current teaching.
- Do not mass-upgrade notebooks, outputs, plotting libraries, or environments without a concrete teaching need.
- Keep generated notebook outputs and large datasets proportionate and intentional.

## Verification boundary

This lifecycle declaration does not certify that every notebook executes from a clean environment or that the published site is current on 2026-08-03.

A bounded review should record:

1. the course or consumer using the material;
2. the representative notebooks opened or executed;
3. environment and dependency assumptions;
4. published-site availability;
5. broken links, missing data, or stale explanations;
6. whether corrections belong here or in a newer teaching repository.

## Authority boundary

This repository owns this notebook collection. It does not define the current curriculum, course administration, or every canonical version of material reused elsewhere.
